This is the Minecraft Bible, restoring the story that it was, but a bad look could not clear the face of the real story and the missing Notch.
Story:
Chapter 1: Creation and the First Player
    1. There was a Notch in the Beginning, and Notch created the First Chunk. And that chunk was beautiful and full of harmony.
    2. And on the First Chunk, Notch created the first of the players, to whom he gave the name Herobrine, so that he would be the guardian of this world.
    3. But loneliness weighed down Herobrine's heart, and he desired power, not friendship.
Chapter 2: The Coming of New Faces and the Fall
    1. And new players appeared in the world, created by Notch's will. And they began to build houses, erect churches, and live in peace.
    2. When Herobrine saw this, he was filled with anger and envy. And he began to destroy their buildings, kill the players themselves and desecrate their shrines.
    3. And the players called out to Notch, begging for protection from the tyranny of the Fallen.
Chapter 3: Judgment and Exile to the Underworld
    1. Notch saw the atrocities of Herobrine, and he was greatly saddened. And Notch said, "For your pride and hatred, you will be banished from the world."
    2. And Notch hurled Herobrine into the Lower World, which was empty, bottomless and dark at that time, so that he would reflect on his sin alone.
Chapter 4: The Corruption of the Underworld
    1. But Herobrine's heart did not repent, but was filled with even greater malice.
    2. He poured out rivers of lava to clear the trail of Notch's creation. The oaks that grew there twisted and turned into ghostly mushrooms.
    3. The pigs, having touched his filth, acquired a new look and mind, becoming piglins. And the most cunning of them learned how to forge weapons out of gold.
    4. And Herobrine created legions of hostile mobs in order to sow fear and suffering.
Chapter 5: The Separation of Souls and the Birth of a New Threat
    1. And there were those among the players who turned away from Notch's face and began to revere the power of Herobrine. Their souls, having left their bodies, fell into his world, taking the form of black skeletons, Witts, which is the very essence of their darkness.
    2. On earth, their bodies rose up in the form of the walking dead — zombies. And those that have decayed to the bone have become skeletons. And from them came all the other kinds of undead.
    3. Those who remained loyal to Notch found peace in the Endworld after death, becoming endermen in a world without time, where there is neither day nor night. And they called this place "Paradise".
    4. The inhabitants, simple villages, unknowingly began to worship creepers, but this was not imputed to them as a sin, because creepers are Notch's creation, albeit unsuccessful.
Chapter 6: The Land of the Apostates and the Great Betrayal
    1. Herobrine, in his exile, was preparing revenge. All the sinners and apostates who have tasted of the pie of the infinite world have split off their piece and named their land 2B2T.
    2. And they began to be called griffins, because they turned away from the face of God and joined the face of Darkness.
    3. So the war lasted for fifteen years.
    4. But in the seventh year, the most grandiose and sad thing happened for the dark face: his minions themselves, driven by greed, raised their hands against their master and stabbed him.
    5. The soul of Herobrine, overflowing with collected sins, disintegrated and merged into the dead sand of souls in the Lower World.
Chapter 7: Reckoning and Eternal War
    1. And now his soul is languishing there, and every soul that he has devoured is suffering with him. But the prophecy says: if the heads of sinful Witt skeletons are attached to him, he will be reborn, weakened, but burning with hatred for all things.
    2. Notch, seeing how faith in him was distorted and created only strife, could not bear this burden. Faith overwhelmed him, and he disintegrated, turning into the legion of Ender-Dragons, in order to soar forever between worlds.
    3. To this day, the two beginnings — the Darkness of Herobrine and the Light of Notch — do not converge in a personal battle, but wage their eternal war through the hearts and hands of all players.

The Commandments:
The Tablets of Notch's Will
Instructions given to players for the Path of Light
The Commandments of the First Tablet: About Creation and the World
    1. May you have no other face before me except the face of the Creator. For apostasy and worship of Darkness will turn your soul to dust and bones.
    2. Do not desire empty destruction. This world is an endless canvas, and your hands are a brush. Destroy only to build again, because destruction for the sake of destruction is the way to Gerobrin.
    3. Build great things and be generous in your creations. Share your knowledge with others so that your buildings inspire, not frighten.
    4. Honor the night and its dangers. Stay in the light or find shelter, for darkness is created by the Fallen and his children roam in it.
The Commandments of the Second Tablet: About Will and Freedom
    5. Do not honor my creatures as gods. Creepers, endermen, and others are part of the world, but not its lords. Honoring them is a distortion of my will.
    6. Keep your free will as your greatest gift. But remember: you are responsible for your choice. Whoever chooses the path of the griefer will choose his fate.
    7. Do not betray your brother. For whoever raises a sword against a trusted one will be like the henchmen who betrayed Herobrine himself.
The Final Word:
"These commandments are not chains, but a foundation. I have given you endless peace and freedom to create. Create with wisdom and mercy, and you will find Paradise at the end of the path. Choose darkness, hatred, and greed, and you will become inhabitants of the decayed soul of the Fallen. The choice is yours, because you are my hands in this world."
— Notch's will to the players.
